# K-Shapped-Final
A data visualization scrollytelling article analyzing consumer expenditure and wealth distribution data from the Bureau of Labor Statistics to identify widening economic disparities across income brackets

CPIAUCSL_PC1_1.csv is the dataset in which the Bureau of Labor Statistics houses inflation data by month from 2016 to April 2026

SP500.csv is the dataset in which we see the current total market value of 500 leading U.S. public companies through 2016 to April 2026

cu-income-quintiles-before-taxes-2024(1).xlxs is a dataset from the 2024 consumer expenditure surveys exploring quintiles of income before taxes with annual expenditure means

data.html is the output from data.qmd showing my sources and info on the article

dfa-networth-levels.csv dataset from the Federal Reserve, sourced from the Survey of Consumer Finances and Financial Accounts of the United States. Networth across levels

economy.png is the image that makes up the background in the article

index.html is the output of the main Quarto document (index.qmd), where the cleaning, visualization, and scrollytelling syntax was developed

sp500_animated.gif is the output of the 1st plot to have animate as part of the article. This was done to avoid having to render every time, as it got expensive.
